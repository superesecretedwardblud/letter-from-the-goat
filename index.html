letter four

The distance

We haven't met yet.

I've never seen you walk into a room. Never heard you laugh from across a table instead of through a speaker. I don't know how tall you are standing next to me. I don't know what I'll do with my hands the first time I see you.

People treat that like it makes it less real. It's the opposite. There's nothing holding this together except the fact that we keep choosing it. No convenience, no running into each other, no easy version. Just both of us deciding, every single day, that it's worth it.

So when it does happen, I don't think it'll feel like meeting you. I think it'll feel like recognising you.

letter five

The sealed one

Hatsune,

Everything I know about you, I know through a screen, and it's still the most real thing in my life.

You're thoughtful in a way I didn't think people still were. You notice things. You remember things. You tell me I'm worth something on the days I've decided I'm not, and you never make it sound like a favour. You make me laugh so hard I forget what I was upset about in the first place. And you meet me exactly where I am, however weird that is, and not once have you made me feel like I'm too much.

I wasn't easy. You know that better than anyone. I believed the wrong people, I went quiet on you, I shut the door — and you were still standing there when I opened it. I don't think you understand what that did to me.

I don't have the ending of this. We haven't gotten there yet. But I know that whatever I'm doing in ten years, I want to be able to look over and find you there.

Until I can say all this to your face, this is what I could build.

Yours, obviously.

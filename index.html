letter one

How much I miss you

More than I planned on.

I thought missing you would be a thing that happened at night, on a schedule, and then stopped. It isn't. It's the middle of the afternoon. It's finding something stupid and having nowhere to put it. It's the half second before I remember.

Everything still routes through you. Something good happens and my first thought is telling you. Something bad happens and my first thought is telling you. The thought arrives before the remembering does, every time, and then I have to put it down again.

I don't know how to explain how loud a person's absence can be. You're not here and somehow you're in everything.

letter two

What I got wrong

None of this makes me look good. It's staying in.

one

Your friends were fighting with you, so they came to me. They said things about you that weren't true, and I believed them. That's the part I have to sit with. Not that they lied, but that it worked.

two

What I did next was worse. I pulled away. Went cold. Made it hard on purpose. You had done absolutely nothing, and you were the one holding it together while I decided whether to take strangers at their word over yours.

three

I blocked you. I want that written down somewhere permanent, because you deserved better and I'd rather own it than pretend it never happened. You were still there afterward. I'm still not over that.

four

And I did the thing where I pretended I was done, more than once, because some part of me needed to watch you talk me out of it. That wasn't fair. You paid for it every time.

what I've decided about all of it

You were telling the truth the entire time. When I finally worked that out, you didn't hold it over me once. Not one time. You just let me come back. I'm not asking you to do that again on the strength of a website. I just needed you to know I understand exactly what I did.

letter four

Every day

You've written every day.

I've read all of them. The long ones. The ones sent at 2am. The ones where you were just telling me about your day like nothing had changed. I've read every single one and answered none, and I need you to understand that the silence was never me not caring. It was the only thing I had that proved this was serious.

You asked once if writing every day was too much. It wasn't. It's the reason I've been able to stand this at all.

You also said you're scared I'm forgetting you a little each day. That's the one thing in all of this you've got completely wrong.

letter five

When this is over

Hatsune,

I don't think the break was the right call.

I've thought about it every day since and I can't find the version where it helped. I wanted it to fix something. Mostly it's just been me missing you with nothing to show for it.

I want it to be over. I want to text you about nothing. I want to hear about your day, and the tests you did well on, and whatever weird thing happened to you at 11pm. I want it back so badly that it's embarrassing.

But I'm the one who asked for this, so I don't get to be the one who breaks it early because it got uncomfortable. That's not fairness. It's just the least I can do.

What I feel worst about is the waiting. You're sitting there every day writing into nothing, and I put you there. You didn't ask for it. And I know exactly what it costs you, because it's costing me the same thing.

There's one more thing, and I've rewritten it about six times.

I don't want to lose you. I want to be clear about that first, because everything after it is going to sound like the opposite.

But I don't want you spending the rest of these three months waiting on someone who can't even answer you. And if someone comes along who's good to you, who's actually there, who treats you the way you should have been treated this whole time, I'm not going to stand in the way of it. Not because I don't care. Because I do, more than I know how to write down, and wanting good things for you has to include the ones that aren't me.

I hope it's me. I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

I miss you. I regret it. I'm still counting.

Yours. Still.
